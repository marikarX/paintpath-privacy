1. A painting studio, without an account

PaintPath is a painting and learning app provided by Synapse One. This policy covers the PaintPath Android app and explains its current data practices. You do not need to create a PaintPath account to paint.

PaintPath does not include advertising, behavioral tracking or a developer-operated analytics service. Synapse One does not operate a server that receives your paintings or painting progress, and does not sell your personal data.

2. Your artwork and progress

The app stores paintings, brushstroke history, lesson progress, settings, recent color mixes, gallery thumbnails, frame selections and painting-process recordings in its private storage on your device. These files let you continue painting, revisit your work and create exports.

PaintPath uses these files locally. The app does not automatically publish your artwork or send it to Synapse One. Device backup and sharing that you initiate are described below.

3. Android device backup

PaintPath supports Android's system backup and restore. Depending on your device settings, backup provider and Android version, eligible app data may be backed up to your Google account or transferred when you move to another device.

Eligible data includes settings, lesson progress, paintings and frame selections. Painting checkpoints, process recordings, timelapse files, exported images and the local purchase cache are excluded from PaintPath's backup selection. Storage limits can mean only some paintings are backed up. Purchases are restored through Google Play.

Synapse One cannot access your Android backup. Manage backup, restore and stored backup data through your device and Google account settings. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. One-time purchases

Optional content packs and gallery frames use Google Play Billing. Google processes payment under its own terms and privacy policy. PaintPath does not receive your credit-card number, bank details or billing address.

The app receives product and purchase information, including purchase tokens and purchase status, to confirm payment, acknowledge purchases, unlock content and restore purchases. It stores a local record of unlocked content so previously confirmed purchases can remain available offline. Billing requests are exchanged with Google Play; the app does not send purchase tokens to a Synapse One server.

Google may retain transaction records under its own policies. Removing PaintPath's local data does not cancel a purchase, remove Google's transaction records or issue a refund. Manage purchases and refund requests through Google Play.

5. Exporting and sharing your work

When you export an image or painting-process video, PaintPath creates a file on your device. When you choose to share it, Android opens the system share interface and passes the selected file and accompanying text to the app or recipient you choose.

Sharing is your choice. The receiving service's privacy rules apply, and public recipients may be able to view or copy your artwork. Exported files can remain in your device's photo or media library after you uninstall PaintPath; manage those copies in your gallery or file manager.

6. Support and third-party services

If you email Synapse One, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot your request and maintain necessary support records. Do not send payment credentials or other sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Email is handled by our email service provider. Google independently handles Play payments, Android backup and Google Play services such as store reviews. Their policies apply to those services. Android and Google Play may also process platform diagnostics under your device and account settings.

This policy webpage is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it involves ordinary web requests to GitHub, which may process technical information such as your IP address; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. This page uses no advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms.

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • App-private paintings and progress remain on your device until you delete them, clear PaintPath's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Clearing app storage removes local progress and cached unlocks. Previously purchased products can be restored through Google Play.
  • Manage backed-up copies separately through Android and your backup provider; clearing local storage alone may not remove an existing backup.
  • Delete exported images and videos separately using your gallery or file manager. Copies already shared with other services are controlled by those services.
  • For support correspondence or a privacy request, contact the email below. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle support, meet applicable obligations and resolve disputes. We can delete information under our control, but cannot delete your Google purchase history, device backups or copies held by recipients.

The app's private storage is managed by Android. Google Play and backup services provide their own transport and account protections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Audience and policy updates

PaintPath is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

We will update this page when PaintPath's data practices change and show the effective date above. New data-handling features will be described before they are released.
